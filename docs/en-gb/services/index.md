We build business systems, internal tools and integrations to fit the way your
organisation already works — and then we keep them running. Most of our
engagements turn into long relationships rather than one-off deliveries.

## Where We Fit

We are not the right choice for every project, and it saves everybody time to
say so before we start.

### A good fit

- **The business logic is the hard part.** You need something shaped around how
  you actually operate, not a template with your logo dropped into it.
- **You have no in-house engineering team**, or the one you have is committed
  elsewhere.
- **You care what happens after launch.** We would rather maintain a system for
  years than hand it over and disappear.
