**Last updated: 25 September 2026.**

This policy covers this website and the tools we publish under it. It is issued
by Changsha Onix Byte Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us"), the operator of
onixbyte.com and onixbyte.cn.

## What we collect

Almost nothing.

There is no account to create, no newsletter to sign up for and no form to
submit. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts of any kind,
and we do not build profiles of visitors.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal
information, and there is nowhere on this site to give it to us.

## Tools that run in your browser

The tools we publish, including Dev Lab, run entirely in your browser. Data you
paste into them — configuration files, API responses, anything else — is
processed on your own machine and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
There is no upload step, and no code in those tools that could perform one.

This is a deliberate design decision. We use these tools on real data
ourselves, and a tool that sends your data somewhere is a tool we would not be
able to use.

## Local storage, and no cookies

We do not set cookies.

The site does store two small pieces of information in your browser's local
storage. Both stay on your device, and neither is readable by us:

| Key                        | What it holds                                     |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `rspress-theme-appearance` | Whether you chose the light or dark theme         |
| `rspress-visited`          | A flag so the language redirect only happens once |

Clearing your browser's site data removes both, and the site continues to work
without them.

## Server logs

Like any web server, the hosts that serve this site may record standard access
logs — IP address, browser user agent, and the page requested — for security and
troubleshooting. These are not used to identify or profile individuals, and we
do not combine them with any other information.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is generally
nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you believe we hold something
about you, write to us and we will look into it.

If you are in a jurisdiction that grants you rights over your personal data,
those rights are not affected by anything in this policy.
